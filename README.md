Limintations:
- Does not cover every construction site in NYC. Only uses public data from NYC Department of Design and Construction (DDC)
- Data is not real-time
- Noise and dust exposure levels are estimates, not direct measurements or health assessments
- Cannot guarantee exposure-free routes.
